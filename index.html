04 / Get consistent measurements

  1. Keep your face fully visible and look toward the camera.
  2. Place the camera around eye level; avoid very close wide-angle selfies.
  3. Use even light and avoid filters, heavy shadows, and face coverings.
  4. Repeat with another image if the landmark overlay looks misplaced.
  5. Use Clear when finished, especially on a shared phone.
