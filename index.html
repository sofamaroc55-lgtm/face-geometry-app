03 / Get a more consistent result

  1. Use a front-facing image with the full face visible.
  2. Keep the camera near eye level and avoid strong perspective distortion.
  3. Use even lighting and a neutral expression where possible.
  4. Try another image if the landmark overlay misses the face.
